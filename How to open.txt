After Bell for Mac
==================

This is a native macOS app (SwiftUI + SceneKit). It is not for iPhone.

1. Unzip this folder if needed.
2. Double-click AfterBell.xcodeproj  (not the .swift files, not Qt).
3. If Xcode asks to Trust / Open, choose Trust.
4. At the top of Xcode, set the destination to My Mac
   (not an iPhone simulator).
5. Press the Play button, or Command-R.

Needs macOS 14 or later and Xcode 15+.

First launch may take a minute to compile. After that the app window is After Bell:
glazed 3D subject bricks, homework list, manage subjects, and Choose room photo.

Your list is saved on this Mac in Application Support/AfterBell.
